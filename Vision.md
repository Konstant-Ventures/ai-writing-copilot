# AI Writing Copilot — Product Vision

**Status:** Draft / In Discovery
**Last updated:** May 31, 2026
**Tagline:** *Your personal team of writing agents.*

---

## The Core Insight

Most AI writing tools follow one of two patterns, but they rarely do both well, and they never let *you* stay in control.

| Pattern | Metaphor | What It Does | Problem |
|---------|----------|--------------|---------|
| AI writes for you | Ghost Writer | "Write a blog post about X" → full draft | You lose control over style, structure, voice |
| AI helps you write | Editor | "Improve this paragraph" → suggestions | No deep awareness of what you're building or why |

**The vision:** An AI Writing Copilot that gives you a *team of agents* — each with a clear role — so you stay in the driver's seat while getting expert help at every stage of writing.

**Core philosophy:** Writing is a craft. The tool should amplify the writer, not replace them. Every agent has a sharply defined role. When boundaries blur, the user gets confused and the system breaks down.

---

## The Team

| Agent | Role | Metaphor |
|-------|------|----------|
| **Sounding Board** | Takes a messy thought and helps you figure out what to write, in what format, for whom | A trusted colleague you bounce ideas off of |
| **Ghost Writer** | Drafts text for you, within boundaries you define (Style Profile) | A writer who follows your style guide |
| **Editor** | Reads your work and tells you what to improve — structurally, logically, stylistically | A senior editor who marks up your draft |
| **Style Architect** | Helps you define, refine, and manage your Style Profiles | A writing coach who learns your voice |
